TexContractor for AI assistants

Ask your AI assistant for a local pro, and it can search TexContractor for you. Free for homeowners. No lead fees for pros.

What it does

ToolWhat it doesExample
find_local_prosFinds independent home-service pros listed on TexContractor by service and city. Returns names, service, city and a link to each profile, where you see work photos and text the pro."Find me a plumber in Arlington, TX"
post_job_requestGives you a link to post your job so nearby pros contact you. Set urgent for ⚡ BOOM: your job is sent straight to pros near you and stays open 24 hours. Nothing is posted until you confirm on texcontractor.com."My AC stopped, I need someone today in Dallas"
list_open_job_requestsFor pros: open jobs homeowners posted, by state, city or service."Any handyman jobs in Houston?"
list_servicesAll service categories on TexContractor."What services does TexContractor cover?"

All tools are read-only. They never change anything and never share phone numbers.

Add it to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name: TexContractor. URL:https://mcp.texcontractor.com/mcp
  3. No sign-in is needed. Save, then ask: "Find me a garage door repair pro in Dallas, TX."

Technical details: remote MCP server, Streamable HTTP transport, no authentication, public read-only data.

Privacy

The connector only reads information already public on texcontractor.com. It does not collect your name, phone number, email or messages. Your search words (like the service and city) are used only to answer that search. Read the full privacy policy.
