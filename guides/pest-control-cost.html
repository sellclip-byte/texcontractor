How Much Does Pest Control Cost?

Updated November 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

Pest control prices depend on the pest, how big the problem is, the size of your home and whether you choose a one-time visit or a recurring plan. These national figures help you know what to expect before you call exterminators near you.

Typical cost

One guide puts the national average at about $223, with most one-time visits between $158 and $288. Another reports an average of $171 to treat a standard infestation, with most visits between $50 and $500.

What to ask before you hire

  • What pest is it, and how will you treat it?
  • Is the first visit priced differently from follow-up visits?
  • If I sign up for a plan, can I cancel any time?
  • Are the products safe for kids and pets, and how long should we stay out?
  • If the pests come back between visits, do you return at no extra charge?
