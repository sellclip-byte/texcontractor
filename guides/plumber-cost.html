How Much Does a Plumber Cost?

Updated October 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

Most plumbing jobs are priced by the hour or as a flat rate per job, often with a service call or trip fee. Knowing the usual ranges helps you compare quotes from plumbers near you.

Typical rates

ItemTypical range
Average plumbing visitAbout $336 (most pay $180 – $494)
Hourly rateUsually $70 – $120 per hour (full range $45 – $200)
Service call fee$100 – $250, often including the first hour
Evenings, weekends, holidaysAbout 1.5× to 3× the normal hourly rate

How to get a clear price

  • Describe the problem and send photos before the visit.
  • Ask if the service call fee is applied to the repair.
  • Ask for the price in writing before work starts.
  • Ask whether parts are included and what warranty applies.
  • For a leak, shut off the water at the main valve if it is safe to do so.
