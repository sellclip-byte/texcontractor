How Much Does Garage Door Repair Cost?

Updated November 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

A garage door that will not open is usually caused by a broken spring, a loose cable, a bent track or an opener problem. The price depends on which part failed and how fast you need it fixed. These national figures help you compare quotes from garage door technicians near you.

Typical total cost

One guide reports an average garage door repair of about $479, with a typical range of $98 – $860. Another puts the average at about $300, with most repairs between $100 and $600.

Example: one guide breaks down a basic spring replacement at about $360: $108 in parts, $192 in labor and a $60 service call. Your real price depends on your door and your area — always get a written quote.

Service call, labor and emergencies

  • Service call fee: about $50 – $75, often waived if you approve the repair.
  • Labor: about $60 per hour on average, and $150 or more per hour with some companies.
  • Emergency or weekend work costs more — one guide notes a $300 spring job can double on an emergency weekend call.

Safety first

Garage door springs hold a lot of tension and can cause serious injury. If a spring or cable is broken, keep the door closed, do not try to lift it by hand, and let a technician do the repair.

What to ask before you hire

  • What is the service call fee, and is it waived if I approve the repair?
  • Is the price in writing, with parts and labor separated?
  • If one spring broke, should both springs be replaced?
  • Is there an extra charge for same-day or weekend service?
  • What warranty covers the parts and the labor?

If the repair is close to the price of a new door (about $700 – $2,500 according to one guide), ask for a replacement quote too.
