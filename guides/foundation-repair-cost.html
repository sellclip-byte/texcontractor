How Much Does Foundation Repair Cost?

Updated November 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

Foundation repair prices depend on what is wrong, the type of foundation and how much of the house is affected. Small cracks can cost a few hundred dollars, while piers or lifting a house can cost tens of thousands. These national figures help you know what to expect before you call foundation pros near you.

Typical total cost

One guide puts the national average at about $5,200, with most jobs between $2,200 and $8,200. Another reports a 2026 average of $5,179, with a typical range of $2,224 – $8,134.

Signs to get checked: cracks wider than a coin, doors or windows that stick, sloping floors, gaps around window frames, or water in the basement or crawl space. Small problems usually cost much less to fix early.

What to ask before you hire

  • Do you recommend a structural engineer report before the work?
  • What method will you use (piers, slabjacking, wall stabilization) and why?
  • Is the price per pier or for the whole job, in writing?
  • Is a permit needed, and who gets it?
  • What warranty covers the work, and can it transfer if I sell the house?
