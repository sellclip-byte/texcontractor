How Much Does an Electrician Cost?

Updated November 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

Electricians charge by the hour or by the job, and the price depends on the work, the electrician's experience and whether a permit is needed. These national figures help you know what to expect before you call electricians near you.

Typical cost

One guide puts the average electrical job at about $300, with most jobs between $150 and $600. Bigger projects can run $2,000 to $10,000.

Hourly rates

ElectricianTypical hourly rate
Apprentice$40 – $60
Journeyman$60 – $90
Master$90 – $120

Some electricians charge a minimum of one to four hours, and an emergency or after-hours call can add at least $100 to $200.

Permits

Bigger jobs like a new panel or new circuits often need a permit, which can cost about $75 to $900 depending on your city.

What to ask before you hire

  • Do you charge by the hour or a flat price for this job?
  • Is there a minimum charge or a trip fee?
  • Does this job need a permit, and will you pull it?
  • Is the price for parts and labor together?
  • Do you guarantee your work?
