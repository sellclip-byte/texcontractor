How Much Does House Cleaning Cost?

Updated November 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

House cleaners usually charge a flat price per visit, based on the size of the home and the type of cleaning, and some charge by the hour. These national figures help you compare quotes from house cleaners near you.

Typical price per visit

One guide puts the national average at $185 – $324 per cleaning, with low-end jobs around $124 and high-end jobs around $493. Another reports that a standard one-time cleaning usually costs $140 – $220, and that cleaners often charge $30 – $80 per hour per cleaner.

How to get a clear price

  • Tell the cleaner the number of bedrooms, bathrooms and the square feet.
  • Say if it is a standard, deep or move-out cleaning.
  • List any extras (oven, fridge, cabinets, windows) before the visit.
  • Ask what is included and whether they bring their own supplies.
  • Ask for a lower price if you book every week or every 2 weeks.
