How Much Does Furnace Repair Cost?

Updated November 2026. Prices are national estimates from the sources below and change by location and over time.

When the heat stops working, the repair bill depends on what failed, the type of furnace and the parts it needs. These national figures help you know what to expect before you call heating pros near you.

Typical cost

One guide puts the national average at about $485, with most repairs between $349 and $650. Another reports an average of $450, with most repairs between $300 and $1,200. Labor usually runs about $87 to $162 per hour.

Repair or replace?

Furnaces usually last about 15 to 20 years, and a new one can cost about $2,000 to $10,000. If your furnace is near the end of its life and needs an expensive part like a heat exchanger, ask for a replacement quote too.

What to ask before you hire

  • What part failed, and can you show me?
  • Is the service call fee included in the repair price?
  • Is the part covered by a warranty?
  • Is this a safety problem, like a cracked heat exchanger or a gas leak?
  • Should I repair it, or is replacing it the better deal at this age?
