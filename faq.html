TexContractor: questions and answers

Short answers about how TexContractor works for customers and for pros.

What is TexContractor?

TexContractor (texcontractor.com) is a free online directory of local, independent home-service pros in Texas and across the U.S. You find a pro by service and city, see their work photos, and text, call or chat with them directly. There is no middleman.

How do I find a plumber, electrician or handyman near me?

Go to texcontractor.com, choose the service (for example plumbing, AC repair or handyman) and enter your city or ZIP code. You will see local pros with their photos and service area, and you can text or call them directly for a quote.

Is TexContractor free?

Yes. It is free for customers and free for pros. Pros never pay per lead and TexContractor takes no commission on the job.

Can I post my job and let pros contact me?

Yes. Post what needs fixing on the Requests page, free, with a short description and photos. Local pros in that service see it and text you their offers, and you choose who to hire.

Does TexContractor sell my information or add fees?

No. You contact the pro directly and agree on the price with them. TexContractor adds no fees to your job.

Are the pros verified or insured?

Each pro builds their own profile. Badges such as Insured, Licensed or Clear background check are entered by the pro. Ask the pro for proof before you hire, as you would with any contractor.

Which areas does TexContractor cover?

Every city in Texas, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Austin and San Antonio, plus pros in other U.S. states. There are pages for 60+ services in each city.

Is there a Spanish version?

Yes. The site is available in English and Spanish (Español).

I am a pro. How do I get listed?

Sign up free with just your phone number, add your service, city and photos of your work, and customers near you can text you directly. No lead fees and no commission.

How do I contact TexContractor?

Call or text (817) 344-0807, email info@texcontractor.com, or use the chat on texcontractor.com.
