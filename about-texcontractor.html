How TexContractor works

Three simple ways to connect

TexContractor is a local service directory for independent technicians and the people who need them. Customers deal with the technician directly — no middleman.

🔍

Find a local technician

Search by service and city, look at each technician's profile and work photos, then call, text or chat with them directly for a quote.

Browse services →
NEW📋

Customer Requests

Describe the job, add up to 4 photos, and post your request. Local technicians who offer that service in your state send you offers — up to 5 per request.

  • You choose how they contact you: chat, text, or call.
  • With chat, your phone number stays private.
  • Reply to the offer you like, then close your request.
Post or see requests →

For technicians

List your service with your city, photos of your work and how customers can reach you. Customers find you in search, and you can send offers on Customer Requests that match your service and state.
