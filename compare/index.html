TexContractor vs Angi, Thumbtack, Yelp & Craigslist

Why pay to reach customers who are already around you? Here is how each site works for local service pros and for customers, using what each company says on its own pages.

How pros payHow customers reach the pro
TexContractorListing your service is free. No per-lead fees and no commission.Customers find you by service and city, then text you directly. No middleman, no bidding against other pros for a lead.
AngiPros are charged per lead, from a monthly budget they set. Angi says lead prices vary by task, location and demand.Angi matches homeowners with pros and sends the pro the lead.
ThumbtackPros pay for leads. Thumbtack lets pros set a maximum lead price in their settings.Customers request quotes and pros pay to receive those leads.
CraigslistPosting in the “services offered” section costs $5 per post in the US.Customers browse posts by city and contact the poster. Posts expire and have to be posted again.
YelpA basic Yelp business page is free. Yelp sells advertising to show a business more often.Customers read reviews and contact the business through Yelp or directly.
