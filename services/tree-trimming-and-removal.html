Tree Trimming and Removal Contractors

Tree services can involve pruning overgrown limbs, removing dead branches, storm cleanup, or assessing trees that lean toward buildings and power lines.

Describe the tree and access conditions

Tell the provider the approximate tree size, proximity to structures, access for equipment, and whether debris removal or stump work is needed. Ask about insurance, permits where required, and cleanup.

Tree work safety

Do not approach fallen power lines or attempt to cut suspended limbs. Large-tree removal and work near utility lines require appropriately equipped professionals.
