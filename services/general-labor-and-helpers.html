General Labor and Helpers

Find independent workers for general tasks: loading and unloading trucks or trailers, carrying boxes and materials, cleanup after a project, clearing a garage or yard, and other simple help by the hour or by the job. Individuals and independent workers only — no companies.

Details to include in your request

Describe the task, how many helpers you need, how many hours you expect, the location, stairs, and any heavy items. Photos help the worker give you a fair price.

Planning and safety

This category is for general, non-specialized work. Jobs that need a license or a specialist — electrical, plumbing, roofing, tree climbing — belong in their own service. Agree on the price and hours before the work starts.
